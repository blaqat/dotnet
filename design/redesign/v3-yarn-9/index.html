The write-up editor

S3 · pins 3, 14

id like it to just be a live markdown editor like notion with blocks … tho u can have button to view raw markdown

Every markdown field is a block editor in the site's own look. Markdown shortcuts as you type; / or + inserts yarn blocks: your link component, images, a song or playlist, a mention, a sticky note, a post card. Each one is edited in place. Blocks | Markdown shows the exact text it saves.

Links: any link, your own text

S5 · pins 5, 11, 15, 18

i should be able to give a list any of arbitrary links and the ones we recognize we convert

One links field everywhere a link lives: songs, collection tracks, the collection itself (its album or playlist), projects, image posts, picks, Home. Paste one or several; SoundCloud, Spotify, Bandcamp, YouTube, Apple Music, GitHub, itch.io and BeepBox get their mark and a clean address. Each link takes your own text, reorders and removes with Undo. Previews show them as the site would.

Playlists from anywhere

S2 · pin 2

playlists to support not just my music but soundcloud bandcamp spotify and youtube so i can post my yearly music roundups

Paste SoundCloud, Spotify, Bandcamp or YouTube links into a playlist, next to your own songs. Title and artist are guessed from the address and editable. On the post, ▶ plays an outside track in its row with that service's player (Bandcamp opens its page); your own songs play in the site's player.

Tracks are songs · mixed instruments

S6, S9 · pins 6, 10

collection items can show up as singles and also have the same metadata a normal song does

A collection track has every field a single has: audio, instrument, status, started, finished, links, notes (one song form for both). The collection's status and dates come from its tracks and say which. A mixed collection's yarn is striped by instrument and its facts say “DAW + Piano”.

The Music order on the site

S10 · pin 12

how do i set the order of how it shows on the site … or by end date which i think is what my old site did

“Order on the site” on the Music list: Newest finished (your old site's rule: WIP on top, Complete by its finish, Dropped by its finish or else its start), Newest started, A–Z, or By hand. The list is the site's order, with its year pills; a change shows in History.
