Independent review: what to keep

A separate reviewer rendered every page at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 px, tabbed through each one, measured contrast, and tested reduced motion. Verdict: the yarn direction works and still reads as minimal. Desktop Projects and the player are the strongest pieces. Every variant is still here; these are recommendations, not cuts.

Keep: the Drop intro (first visit), the Simple intro (returning visits) · Projects list + stage, the grid toggle, rows on mobile · the Swatch library on desktop, Ledger on mobile, the player panel over its bar · Stitched Thoughts, masonry plus the thread layout · the yarn tier list · corduroy for the mobile nav.

Consider cutting: the Cursor intro, which takes the cat out of the lockup · the Deck on mobile (one project per screen) · the Dock player, which squeezes the library · Ethereal as a whole site section (keep its red-thread timeline and italic scratch quotes as ideas) · the suede and burlap nav.

Fixed after review

  • Contact in the nav now opens Home's contact links; it used to be a dead link on section pages.
  • Kit: yarn red darkened to #d62f38 so white text passes AA; burlap darkened; focus rings restored on stitched buttons and chips; chips are 44 px tall on touch; mobile nav labels enlarged.
  • Tier list: now includes the global player (night theme on the ethereal skin); the selected tab no longer flashes white-on-cream.
  • Ethereal: the night stitch frame is now the default (the gold cord read as mud).
  • Player: on mobile the mini player is merged into the brown nav dock, cutting the bottom chrome from 161 to 121 px. The play button's keyboard focus is visible again. The mobile sheet is a proper modal while open. There's a night theme for dark pages. Music defaults to the Ledger library on mobile.
  • Home: the stalking cat no longer covers the "b", and its tail stays inside the frame on mobile. Skip is 44 px and appears after 600 ms but is keyboard-reachable from the start. Notes fly lower and wider, behind the bio text. The duplicate bottom bar is hidden on home, where the tiles already are the nav.
  • Thoughts · Stitched: every tag, date and chip has a 44 px tap area on touch screens. The mobile header is one row, with collections moved into a Browse sheet, so the first post starts at 216 px. Deep-linked lightboxes now close back into their thumbnail.
  • Projects: no more black "video" boxes. Projects without images get an initials badge on blue cord. Rows flow into two columns between 640 and 959 px. The stuck filter strip gets an edge. Every control is 44 px on touch screens.
