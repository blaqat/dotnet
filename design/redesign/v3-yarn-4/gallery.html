What changed in iteration 4

  • using the red fill for the toggleablesDecided: filters you switch on fill red everywhere; single choices keep the rope. "Show all" is "All", and turns into ✕ inside the phone search.
  • the mute button also doesnt fitA woven sound tag hangs from the top of the sheet on every page (?soundui=brand|nav to compare), and Home asks "Press unmute for sound" before the intro.
  • the link openers can background fade into its website's colorGitHub, YouTube, BeepBox, SoundCloud and friends fade to their colour on hover; unknown sites keep the plain button.
  • fix up projects than replicate on musicProjects got interruptible filtering, open in place or as a page, year sections and a list by year on phones; Music copies all of it and keeps its folders.
  • the search bar should expand the same way the music doesOn phones the search button is the default and shares one slot above the bottom bar with the player: the open one fills it, the other waits above.
  • make sure we are using grids when placing the items on screenPage edges, titles, year rules and columns are measured to line up across pages and views at 1440, 1024 and 390.
  • think im leaning on jersy for the backgroundJersey is the default sheet; intarsia is one click away (?bg=intarsia).
