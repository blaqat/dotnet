What changed in iteration 6

  • lets have these floating elements on their own plane of existancePill, search, chips, Browse, player, toasts and hints share one fixed top layer; nothing in the page draws over them, and phones never scroll sideways. The Browse → SoundCloud mess came from hidden folder tabs widening the page by 41px.
  • if label first we can put the action buttons on the bottom right“Label first” is the opened item's default, links bottom right, no gap; a left rail like articles is new (?dlayout=side). Notes in LAYOUT-i6.md.
  • i also kinda like how it looks on the opened card with the icon in the circleCards use those facts, type · status · date (?cardmeta=facts|badges).
  • when you press down on the inner buttons it shouldnt play the press animation for the whole containerA kit rule now; hover also no longer flickers at a card's edge.
  • maybe just doing away with the feature is the best optionHiding the bottom items on scroll is off by default (?mhide=all to try it).
