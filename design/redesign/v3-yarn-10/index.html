A colour means one thing

L1 · L5 · M5

if i were to take my glassess of id barely be able to tell whats playing

Red had seven jobs. Now nothing at rest is a solid accent: a chosen option wears the rope, a filter that's on gets a soft fill, and only what's playing is filled. Section (default): purple is music, so the player and anything playing are purple on every page, and each page's own hue only marks where you are. Ink: black thread does the structure, red only means “playing”. Controls are flat at rest; the sheet and cards keep their stitches.

Allo, with its spacing fixed

L6

keep the option for the font change in this iteration as well and make sure spacing is good

Allo's word space is about 60% of Nunito's at heavy weights; that's what closed the gaps in the first preview. Each text role now has its own tracking and word spacing for Allo (word gaps within 2% of Nunito's), and its size is matched to Nunito Sans's x-height. Pick Allo or Allo Round in ⚙.
