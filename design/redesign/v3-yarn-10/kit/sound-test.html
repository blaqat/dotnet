Sounds.

Every sound on the site, synthesized live in WebAudio (no files). Off by default for visitors; this page turns them on with the toggle. Waveforms and spectrograms are rendered offline with a fixed seed so they can be checked by eye.

Home intro sequence

Typical desktop "drop" timeline (YarnSound.INTRO): ball lands, bounces lower, rolls into the dot, letters stitch out, strand settles, the cat pads in, swats twice and sits.

Intensity: ball-bounce at 1 · .55 · .3 · .12

Waveform: min/max per pixel, red line = 10 ms RMS envelope, dashed lines = -6 dBFS, red samples = clipping; quiet sounds are drawn zoomed (×N, labelled). Spectrogram: 0–8 kHz, 46 ms window, gained by the same zoom. Peak/RMS are measured after the master limiter at master volume 0.8.
